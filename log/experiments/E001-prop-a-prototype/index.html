    E001 Missouri Prop A prototype¶

    • Date: autumn 2026
    • Status: run, provisional
    • Schema version: 0.1, then 0.2 after fixes

    Provisional. The coding was done by a model. No record was hand-coded, and no timing exists. Treat every figure here as a hypothesis.

    Hypothesis¶

    The prediction was selective partial hollowing: some provisions kept, others narrowed. The prediction was wrong.

    Setup¶

    The Prop A certified text was split mechanically into 102 chunks, then coded across the nine layers.

    Result¶

    • 102 records. Provenance counts were recorded as 98 / 1 / 3 (category order to be confirmed on audit)
    • Denominator after the provision rule: 67
    • 66 deleted entirely, 1 partially surviving (p003)
    • p003 survives textually but loses its post-2026 indexing, and also depends on a provision that was rewritten, so it narrows through two channels
    • Layer 4 took one value on 99 of 102 records

    Interpretation¶

    Near-total deletion. The case is close to degenerate for testing the interpretive layers, so it cannot show that Layer 3 or 4 discriminate. It did show the schema gaps listed in the friction log.

    Consequence¶

    Schema v0.2. A second case with variation in mechanism is needed. Hand-coding must come before any pipeline evaluation.
