# How we got here

The abandoned directions are recorded because the reasons for abandoning them became the design criteria.

| Direction | Why it was dropped |
|---|---|
| Model-bill diffusion in state AI legislation | A January 2026 ACLU/Brown report covered adjacent ground, and the project moved away from AI as subject matter |
| Ballot title fidelity | Sound and novel (existing work measures readability, not accuracy) but tabled. The provision-decomposition machinery carried forward |
| Post-*Grants Pass* ordinance drafting | The advisor objected that a null result (ordinances stay facially neutral) was predictable, and that text is a weak proxy for effects on people |
| Doctrinal migration after *Grants Pass* | Better, but pivoted away from |
| Alteration of initiatives: measurement paper | Redirected by the advisor toward a browsable resource, with the Frankenstein Variorum as precedent |

## Design criteria that survived

1. The finding must not be predictable from the armchair.
2. A null result must still be a finding.
3. Textual analysis must connect to consequences.
4. Novelty is checked against existing literature before commitment and claimed narrowly.

## Borrowed from the Frankenstein Variorum

The witness model, the collation spine, heatmap-led navigation, Method and Data as first-class pages, and the dated snapshot. What legal text adds is direction: a deletion narrows and an exemption narrows, where a literary variant has no direction. The visualization therefore needs a second channel for it.
