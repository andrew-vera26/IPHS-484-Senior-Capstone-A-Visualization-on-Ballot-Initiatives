# ADR 0006: Diverse-case selection

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-14

## Context
A small non-random set cannot support a claim of representativeness.

## Decision
Cases are selected to span rule regime, domain, form of alteration and outcome. Criteria and exclusions are published. Counter-cases (technical correction, implementation, unaltered control) are mandatory. All eligible cases are machine-coded and about fifteen are hand-verified.

## Consequences
The paper claims coverage of the range of alteration, not a population estimate.
