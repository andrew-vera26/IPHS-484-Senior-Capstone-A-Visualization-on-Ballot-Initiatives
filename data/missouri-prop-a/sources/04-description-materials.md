## 5. Sponsor discrepancy note — RESOLVED (Gap 2)

**Resolution:** The "discrepancy" is not an error — it reflects the normal House-sponsor / Senate-handler structure for a bill that started in the House and was carried through the Senate by a different member. This is confirmed by cross-referencing multiple independent secondary aggregators that read the underlying house.mo.gov/senate.mo.gov data (house.mo.gov's own HTML bill-tracking pages 404'd on every URL pattern tried — see Resolution notes below — so this was triangulated rather than read directly off a single house.mo.gov page):

- **House sponsor (primary):** Representative Sherri Gallick (R–District 62), the original House sponsor who filed HB 567.
- **House co-sponsors:** Representative Chad Perkins (R–District 40), Representative John Hewkin (R–District 120), Representative Ed Lewis.
- **Senate handler:** Senator Mike Bernskoetter, who carried the bill through the Senate — this is why the **Senate's own bill-tracking page** (item 2 above) lists "Bernskoetter" as the name associated with the bill on that chamber's side; it is not a competing sponsor claim, it is the Senate's own point of contact for its chamber's version of the process.
- The bill as finally passed was **House Committee Substitute No. 2 for House Bills Nos. 567, 546, 758 & 958** — i.e., HB 567 was the lead/vehicle bill for a substitute that merged in three other House bills (546, 758, 958), which is also why some aggregators (e.g., BillSponsor.com) describe "4 sponsors" — that count reflects the four merged original House bills' sponsors, not 4 sponsors of HB 567 alone.

Sources triangulated (secondary aggregators reading official MO legislative data, since house.mo.gov's own bill page could not be fetched directly):
- FastDemocracy bill tracker: https://fastdemocracy.com/bill-search/mo/2025/bills/MOB00024358/?report-bill-view=1 — lists Gallick as primary sponsor, Perkins/Hewkin/Lewis as cosponsors, Bernskoetter as Senate Handler.
- Web search aggregation (LegiScan/BillSponsor-sourced) corroborates: Gallick primary House sponsor; Bernskoetter Senate handler; House passage March 13, 2025; Senate passage May 14, 2025; signed by Gov. Kehoe July 10, 2025.
- Missouri Senate bill-tracking page (https://www.senate.mo.gov/BillTracking/Bills/BillInformation?year=2025&billid=9171446) — lists Bernskoetter, consistent with his role as Senate handler rather than House sponsor of record.
- Governor's press release (item 4 above) crediting both Gallick and Bernskoetter is therefore accurate, not an error: it names the House sponsor and the Senate handler together, as is conventional in signing-event coverage.

**Residual caveat:** house.mo.gov's own canonical bill-tracking HTML page — which would show the complete, dated, official sponsor/cosponsor list with House Journal citations — could not be fetched directly in this session (see Resolution notes). The sponsor list above is corroborated by at least two independent secondary sources reading the same underlying official data, and is consistent with the Senate tracking page and governor's press release, so confidence is high, but it has not been verified against the primary house.mo.gov HTML page itself.
